# Boréal Rewards Loyalty Programme Terms

> Pedagogical document written for the 420-977-VA capstone (fictional retailer).

## Joining the programme

Boréal Rewards is free. Any person aged 18 or over with a Canadian delivery address can join from
the "My account" page; customers outside Canada can join but cannot redeem points on international
orders. One membership per person; memberships cannot be shared or transferred. Business accounts
have a separate programme described in their business agreement.

## Earning points

Members earn 1 point for every dollar spent on products, after discounts and before taxes. Points
are not earned on taxes, delivery fees, gift card purchases or the part of an order paid with
points. Points appear in your account when the order is delivered. Members receive a 200-point
bonus in the month of their birthday, and from time to time we run promotions that award extra
points on selected products; the conditions of each promotion are shown on the product page.

## Redeeming points

Every 500 points can be redeemed for $5 off an order. The minimum redemption is 500 points and
points can pay for up to 50% of the value of the products in an order. You choose how many points
to use at checkout. Points cannot be exchanged for cash or gift cards.

## Membership tiers

Your tier depends on the points you earned in the last 12 months and is recalculated on the first
day of every month.

| Tier | Points earned in the last 12 months | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Essentiel | 0 to 499 | Earn and redeem points; birthday bonus |
| Boréal | 500 to 1,499 | Free delivery from $50; early access to seasonal collections |
| Aurore | 1,500 or more | Free delivery on every order; free change-of-mind return labels; priority customer service |

## Point expiry

Points do not expire as long as your account is active. An account is inactive when no order has
been delivered for 12 consecutive months; the points on an inactive account expire at the end of
that 12-month period. We send a reminder by e-mail 30 days before points expire.

## Returns and cancellations

Points earned on products you return or on orders you cancel are removed from your balance. If you
redeemed points on an order and then return part of it, the points are returned to your account in
proportion to the value of the products you returned.

## Quarterly offers and how members are selected

Each quarter, some members receive a personal discount offer (currently 15% off one order). To
decide who receives it, Boréal Marché uses an automated estimate of how likely each customer is to
order again in the next 90 days, based on past orders and delivery region. A member of the marketing
team reviews and approves every offer list before it is sent. The estimate is not used to change
prices, to refuse service or to change your tier. You can ask which information and main factors
were used for your account, have inaccurate information corrected, ask a staff member to review the
decision, or opt out of this profiling while staying a member: see the Privacy Notice.

## Changes to the programme

We may change these terms with 30 days' notice by e-mail and on the website. If we end the
programme, members will have at least 90 days to redeem their points.

## Questions

For questions about your points balance or tier, see "My account → Boréal Rewards" or contact
customer service at support@borealmarche.example or 1-800-555-0142.
