# Returns, Refunds and Quality Guarantee

> Pedagogical document written for the 420-977-VA capstone (fictional retailer).

## Our commitment

We want every order to arrive complete, in good condition and as described. This policy explains
what you can return, how long you have, how refunds are paid and what we do when something arrives
damaged or wrong. It does not limit any right you have under the Québec Consumer Protection Act or
the consumer protection laws of your province.

## Non-perishable products: 30-day returns

You can return non-perishable products (kitchenware, home decor, lighting, bags and wrapping,
stationery, toys and other specialty goods) within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the
product price, provided the product is unused and in its original packaging with its labels. Items
that were opened but are faulty are covered by the damaged and defective items section below, not by
this section. Seasonal decorations bought between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until
January 15 of the following year.

## Perishable products: quality guarantee instead of returns

For food safety reasons we cannot take back perishable products (dairy, fresh bakery, chilled or
frozen specialty foods) or opened food products. Instead, our quality guarantee applies: if a
perishable product arrives spoiled, warm, past its best-before date or otherwise not up to standard,
report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the product and its label. We refund the
product or credit your account, at your choice, without asking you to send the product back.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If a product arrives damaged, does not work, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of
delivery. We send a replacement at no cost or refund you in full, including the share of the
delivery fee for that product. For large or fragile items we may ask for a photo before sending a
replacement. You do not pay return shipping for our mistakes: we e-mail you a prepaid label, or our
van driver collects the item at your next delivery in Montréal and Québec City.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following are final sale: gift cards; personalised or engraved items; products sold in the
"Dernière chance" clearance section; opened personal-care and hygiene items; and perishable products
(which are covered by the quality guarantee instead). Wholesale orders placed through a business
account follow the return terms of the business agreement.

## How to start a return

Sign in, open "My orders", select the order and choose "Return an item". Select the items and the
reason; you receive a return authorisation number and, where applicable, a prepaid label by e-mail.
You can also start a return through the chat assistant or by e-mail at
support@borealmarche.example with your order number (it starts with "BM"). Pack the items securely
with the return authorisation number and drop the parcel at any Canada Post outlet within 14 days of
receiving the authorisation. Returns sent without an authorisation number can take longer to
process.

## Return shipping costs

When you return a product because you changed your mind, a return label costs $7.95 and is deducted
from your refund. Returns are free when the product was damaged, defective or wrong, and for members
at the Aurore tier of the Boréal Rewards programme. International customers pay the return shipping
and any duties for change-of-mind returns.

## Refunds

We inspect returned items within 3 business days of their arrival at our Laval warehouse. The refund
is then issued to the original payment method; your bank or card issuer usually takes 5 to 10
business days to show it. Refunds for orders paid with a gift card are credited back to a gift card.
If you prefer, you can receive store credit instead of a refund: store credit is issued immediately
after inspection and includes a 10% bonus on the refunded amount. Delivery fees are refunded only
when the whole order is returned because of our error.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the item
you want; if the new item is out of stock, our customer service can reserve it for 48 hours.

## Loyalty points on returned items

Boréal Rewards points earned on a returned product are removed from your balance when the refund is
issued. If you used points to pay for part of the order, the points are returned to your account for
the share of the returned products.

## Late or missing refunds

If you have not received your refund 10 business days after the confirmation e-mail, first check
with your bank, then contact customer service with your order number and return authorisation
number. Customer service can trace the refund and, if it was not issued, issue it the same day.
