# Allergen and Food Safety Information

> Pedagogical document written for the 420-977-VA capstone (fictional retailer). It is not medical
> advice.

## Important: read the label

The product label on the package you receive is the authoritative source of ingredient and allergen
information. Suppliers can change recipes; we update product pages within 2 business days of being
notified, but the label always wins. If you have a severe allergy, read the label of every product
before use, every time, and speak with your doctor or allergist about what is safe for you. Our
customer service and our chat assistant can tell you what our policies say, but they cannot give
medical advice or confirm that a product is safe for a specific person.

## Priority food allergens

Health Canada identifies priority food allergens that must be declared on labels: eggs, milk,
mustard, peanuts, crustaceans and molluscs, fish, sesame seeds, soy, sulphites, tree nuts, and wheat
and triticale. Gluten sources must also be declared. Every food product we sell carries a bilingual
(French and English) label that lists these allergens in the ingredient list or in a "Contains"
statement.

## Precautionary "may contain" statements

Some products carry a "may contain" statement, for example "may contain peanuts" or "peut contenir
des arachides". This means the product was made in a facility or on equipment that also handles the
allergen and that cross-contamination cannot be ruled out. We do not remove products with
precautionary statements from the search results, but the allergen filter on the website can hide
them.

## Using the allergen filter

On every category page and in search results, the "Allergens" filter lets you exclude products that
contain, or may contain, one or more priority allergens. The filter uses the information supplied by
the manufacturer. It is a convenience, not a guarantee: always confirm with the label.

## Shared facilities and our specialty range

Our specialty chocolates, baked goods and confectionery come from small Québec and Maritime
producers. Many of these producers work in shared kitchens that also handle tree nuts, peanuts,
wheat, milk and eggs. Unless a product page explicitly says "made in a dedicated allergen-free
facility", assume that cross-contamination is possible. We cannot guarantee that any product is
completely free of an allergen.

## Gluten-free claims

A product described as "gluten-free" meets the Canadian standard: it contains no gluten above
20 parts per million and no intentionally added gluten sources. Oats are labelled gluten-free only
when they come from a specialised supply chain.

## Non-food products

Some non-food items can also matter to people with allergies or sensitivities. Scented candles and
diffusers contain fragrance compounds; some balloons and party items contain natural rubber latex;
some wooden kitchen tools are finished with food-safe oils that can include nut oils. These products
list their materials on the product page.

## Recalls

We follow the recall notices of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA). When a product we sold is
recalled, we remove it from sale immediately and e-mail every customer who bought it in the
affected period within 24 hours, with instructions. You receive a full refund for a recalled
product, whether or not you still have it, and you should not return it unless the notice asks you
to. Recall notices are also posted on the home page.

## Allergic reactions

If you think you are having an allergic reaction, call 911 or your local emergency number
immediately. After the situation is under control, please contact us with the product name, the
lot number on the package and your order number so that we can investigate with the supplier and,
if needed, report to the CFIA.

## Storage and food safety at home

Refrigerate perishable products as soon as they are delivered and respect the storage instructions
on the label. Do not eat chilled products that arrived above 4 °C or whose packaging is damaged:
report them under our quality guarantee (see the Returns policy) and we will refund them.
