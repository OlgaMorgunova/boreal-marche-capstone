# Delivery and Shipping Policy

> Pedagogical document. Boréal Marché is a fictional retailer created for the 420-977-VA capstone;
> the policies below were written to ground the assistant and do not describe a real company.

## Where we deliver

Boréal Marché delivers groceries and specialty goods to addresses in Québec, Ontario and the
Maritime provinces (New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island). Newfoundland and Labrador
is served for non-perishable products only. We do not deliver perishable or refrigerated products
outside Canada. Customers outside Canada can order non-perishable specialty goods (decor, kitchen
and gift items) through our international service described below. We cannot deliver to post office
boxes for orders that contain perishable products, because those orders need a person or a safe
place at the door within a fixed window.

## Delivery times by region

Delivery times are counted in business days (Monday to Friday, excluding statutory holidays) from
the moment your order is confirmed, which is when payment is authorised.

| Region | Perishable orders | Non-perishable orders |
|---|---|---|
| Greater Montréal and Québec City | Next day if ordered before 2:00 p.m. (Eastern) | 1 to 2 business days |
| Rest of Québec | 2 to 3 business days | 2 to 4 business days |
| Ottawa, Toronto and the Golden Horseshoe | 2 business days | 2 to 3 business days |
| Rest of Ontario | 3 business days | 3 to 5 business days |
| New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island | 3 to 4 business days | 4 to 6 business days |
| Newfoundland and Labrador | not available | 5 to 8 business days |
| International (non-perishable only) | not available | 7 to 15 business days |

These are targets, not guarantees. Severe weather, road closures and carrier disruptions can add one
or more days; when that happens we notify you by e-mail and text message as soon as we know.

## Delivery windows

In Greater Montréal and Québec City our own refrigerated vans deliver in three-hour windows, Monday
to Saturday: 8:00–11:00, 11:00–14:00, 14:00–17:00 and 17:00–20:00. You choose the window at
checkout; the earliest available window depends on the order time and on van capacity. You can
change the window up to 10:00 p.m. on the day before delivery from the "My orders" page. Outside
these two areas, orders are shipped by a partner carrier, which delivers during the day without a
choice of window. The tracking link in your confirmation e-mail shows the carrier's estimate.

## Perishable products and cold chain

Perishable products (dairy, fresh bakery, chilled specialty items) travel in insulated boxes with
gel ice packs designed to keep them at or below 4 °C for 48 hours. To make sure a perishable parcel
never sits in a depot over a weekend, perishable orders to the Maritimes and to the rest of Ontario
ship only from Monday to Wednesday. Orders placed on Thursday, Friday or the weekend ship the
following Monday. Please refrigerate perishable products as soon as you receive them. The insulated
boxes are recyclable and the gel packs are non-toxic; you can return boxes to our van drivers in
Montréal and Québec City for reuse.

taxes; gift cards do not count toward it.

## Missed deliveries

If nobody is home during a van delivery, the driver leaves non-perishable products at the door or
in the safe place you indicated at checkout, and takes a photo. Perishable products are never left
unattended: they are brought back to our Laval depot and kept refrigerated. You can book a new
window for the next day for a $5 redelivery fee, or pick the order up at the Laval depot free of
charge within 48 hours. After 48 hours perishable products are donated to a partner food bank and
refunded as store credit, minus the delivery fee. For carrier deliveries, the carrier leaves a
notice and holds the parcel at the nearest pickup point for five business days.

## Tracking your order

Every order receives a confirmation e-mail with an order number that starts with "BM". When the
order leaves our warehouse you receive a tracking link by e-mail and, if you gave us a mobile
number, by text message. For van deliveries in Montréal and Québec City the link shows the van's
position on the day of delivery and a 30-minute arrival estimate.

## Holidays and seasonal cut-offs

We do not deliver on statutory holidays: New Year's Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day / National
Patriots' Day, the Fête nationale du Québec (June 24) in Québec, Canada Day (July 1), Labour Day,
Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and Boxing Day. Orders that would arrive on a holiday arrive on the next
business day. For the end-of-year season, the last day to order for delivery before December 24 is
published on the home page at the beginning of November; it is usually December 18 for the
Maritimes and December 21 for Greater Montréal and Québec City.

## International orders

International orders contain non-perishable specialty goods only. They are shipped by a tracked
international courier. Import duties, taxes and customs fees of the destination country are paid by
the customer on delivery and are not refunded if a parcel is refused. Delivery times are estimates
because customs clearance is outside our control.

## Wholesale and business deliveries

Business accounts that order in wholesale quantities (see the Ordering and Payment Policy) receive
pallet or multi-box shipments by freight carrier. Freight deliveries require a loading area or a
person able to receive the boxes, and are booked by our business desk with a two-hour window.
