# Ordering, Payment and Account Policy

> Pedagogical document written for the 420-977-VA capstone (fictional retailer).

## Placing an order

You can order on the website or in the mobile app, with or without an account; a guest order
cannot earn Boréal Rewards points and its history is not kept in an account. Prices are in Canadian
dollars. The minimum order is $35 before taxes for orders that contain perishable products; there is
no minimum for orders of non-perishable products only. When the order is placed you receive a
confirmation e-mail with an order number starting with "BM".

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit cards, Visa and Mastercard debit cards,
PayPal, Boréal Marché gift cards and Boréal Rewards points. Your card is authorised when you place
the order and charged when the order leaves our warehouse; if a product is unavailable, you are
charged only for what is shipped. We do not accept cash on delivery or cheques, except for approved
business accounts.

## Taxes

Federal GST and Québec QST, or HST in Ontario and the Atlantic provinces, are added at checkout
according to the delivery address. Basic groceries are zero-rated; specialty goods such as
decorations, kitchenware and confectionery are taxable. The tax amount appears on the invoice that
comes with the confirmation e-mail.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change the products, quantities, delivery window or address of an order, or cancel it, from
"My orders" until 10:00 p.m. on the day before delivery for van deliveries, or until the order
status becomes "In preparation" for carrier deliveries. After that, the order can no longer be
changed; you can refuse a carrier parcel or return non-perishable products under the Returns
policy. A cancelled order is refunded in full to the original payment method; any points used are
returned to your account.

## Substitutions

When a product in your order is out of stock on the day of preparation, we can replace it with a
similar product of the same or lower price, if you allowed substitutions for that product at
checkout. Substituted products are clearly marked on the delivery slip; you can refuse a
substitution at the door or return it free of charge within 7 days. If you did not allow
substitutions, the missing product is removed and not charged.

## Prices and promotions

Prices shown at the time you place the order are the prices you pay, even if they change before
delivery. In case of an obvious pricing error, we contact you before preparing the order and you may
cancel it. Promotional codes are applied at checkout, one code per order, and cannot be combined
with the quarterly loyalty offer unless the code says so. We do not price-match other retailers.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are sold in amounts from $25 to $500. In accordance with Québec law they do not expire
and have no inactivity fees. Gift cards can be used for products and delivery fees but not to buy
other gift cards. A lost gift card can be replaced if you have the purchase receipt.

## Business and wholesale accounts

Businesses (cafés, gift shops, offices) can apply for a business account. Business accounts get
wholesale pricing on orders of 24 units or more of the same product, freight delivery, and, after
approval, payment by invoice at 30 days. Business accounts are managed by our business desk at
business@borealmarche.example.

## Your account

You can update your name, addresses, telephone number and communication preferences in "My
account". To close your account, use "My account → Privacy → Close my account" or write to
privacy@borealmarche.example; we confirm the closure within 30 days. Closing your account cancels
your Boréal Rewards membership and any unused points.

## Customer service

Customer service is open Monday to Friday 8:00–20:00 and Saturday 9:00–17:00 (Eastern time), in
French and English, by chat, by e-mail at support@borealmarche.example and by telephone at
1-800-555-0142. Outside these hours the chat assistant answers questions about our policies and your
orders and passes anything it cannot answer to the team for the next business day.
